### 🔥 DAY 1 — Pull Power + Core Stability

**Warm-up (2 rounds)**  
- Dead hang 30s  
- Scapular pull-ups ×8  
- Band pull-aparts ×15  

**Main Work**  
1. **Pull-ups** → 6 sets × 3–5 reps (full ROM, controlled)  
2. **Negative Pull-ups** → 3 × 3 (6–8 sec descent)  
3. **Band Rows** → 3 × 12–15  

**Core**  
- Hanging Knee Raises → 4 × 10–12  
- Hollow Body Hold → 4 × 30–45s  

**Finisher** (3 rounds)  
- Mountain Climbers 30s / High Knees 30s / Rest 30s

---

### 🔥 DAY 2 — Legs & Glutes (Hip Power Focus)

**Warm-up**  
- Leg swings + Hip circles  
- Bodyweight Squats ×15  

**Main Work**  
1. **Bulgarian Split Squats** (rear foot on chair or low parallettes) → 4 × 10–12/leg  
2. **Band Hip Thrusts** (band around knees) → 4 × 15–20  
3. **Single-leg Romanian Deadlifts** (with band) → 3 × 10–12/leg  
4. **Band Squats** (slow tempo) → 3 × 20  

**Skill / Core**  
- L-sit / Tuck Hold on parallettes → 5 × 15–25s  

**Finisher** (4 rounds)  
- Jump Squats ×12  
- Glute Bridges (bodyweight) ×15  
- Rest 30s

---

### 🔥 DAY 4 — Push + Athletic Shoulders

**Warm-up**  
- Wrist prep + Scapular push-ups ×12  
- Band dislocates ×12  

**Main Work**  
1. **Deep Parallettes Push-ups** → 4 × 10–12  
2. **Pike Push-ups** → 4 × 8–10  
3. **Band Overhead Press** → 3 × 12–15  

**Core**  
- Side Plank → 3 × 45s per side  
- Ab Vacuum → 3 × 40s  

**Finisher** (3 rounds)  
- Burpees ×8  
- Plank Shoulder Taps ×20  
- Rest 45s

---

### 🔥 DAY 5 — Pull Volume + Metabolic Conditioning

**Warm-up**  
- Dead hang 40s  
- Band pull-aparts  

**Main Work**  
1. **Pull-ups** → 5 sets × AMRAP (stop 1–2 reps before failure)  
2. **Inverted Rows** (under bar) → 4 × 10–12  
3. **Band Straight-Arm Pulldowns** → 3 × 15  

**Full Body Circuit** (3–4 rounds)  
- Push-ups ×10  
- Squats ×20  
- Hanging Knee Raises ×10  
- Rest 60–75s
